Nina's bundled food database (`Packages/NinaCore/Sources/NinaCore/Resources/foods.json`) is based on:

U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. USDA National Nutrient Database for
Standard Reference, Release 28.

The database is a work of the U.S. government and is in the public domain (CC0 1.0 Universal). It may be
used without restriction. Nina uses the nutrient values, food descriptions and household weights of
SR28; the names, translations, aliases, categories and portions, and the foods computed from recipes or
taken from typical product labels, are Nina's own additions (see `data/README.md`). USDA has not
reviewed or endorsed Nina.

## Open Food Facts

When you scan a barcode, Nina looks up that single product on Open Food Facts at that moment. The data is
available under the Open Database License (ODbL), © Open Food Facts contributors
(https://world.openfoodfacts.org). Nina fetches single products on request; nothing from Open Food Facts
is bundled with the app.
